# Design System & Visual Direction (DESIGN.md)

## 1. Color Palette Tokens (Strict)
- **Primary Rust / Red**: `#C4622D` (Primary CTA & Pill Button)
- **Secondary Terracotta**: `#D4845A` (Warm Accent)
- **Sand / Divider**: `#E8B89A` (1px Section Dividers & Frame Borders)
- **Cream**: `#F5DEC8` (Section 3 Background & Light Text)
- **Deep Oak Dark**: `#1A1008` (Page Background & Dark Pill Button)

## 2. Typography Scale
- **Headlines & Display**: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display`
- **Body, Nav & Microcopy**: `Inter`

## 3. Motion & Layout Rules
- **ScrollTrigger**: GSAP ScrollTrigger `scrub: 1.5` on all scroll sequences.
- **Drop Shadows**: Strictly none. Contrast achieved via borders and whitespace.
- **Dividers**: `1px solid #E8B89A` between all sections.
- **Section Padding**: Minimum `96px` vertical padding (`py-24`).
